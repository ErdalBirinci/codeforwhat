Cookie policy

Version 2.0. Last reviewed November 2026.

This policy lists the storage this site actually sets. It is short because the list is short: there are no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies and no third-party cookies on any page of this service.

1. What is stored, and why

Name Type Purpose Lifetime
cfw-consent-v1 Local storage Records the choice you made in the consent banner, so the banner is not shown on every page. Strictly necessary to respect the choice itself. 12 months, or until you clear site data.
cfw-theme Local storage The light, dark or system appearance you selected. Strictly necessary to render the page the way you asked for it. Until you clear site data.
cfw-locale Local storage The language you selected, so the next visit is in the same language. Strictly necessary for the interface you chose. Until you clear site data.
Payment session Cookie, set by Stripe on its own domain Fraud prevention during checkout. It is set by the payment processor when you reach its payment page, not by this site, and it is governed by Stripe's own notice. Session to 30 days, per Stripe's published terms.

Nothing in the first three rows leaves your browser: the consent choice, the theme and the language are read by the page and are not sent to our server.

2. What is not used

  • No analytics or statistics service, and no session recording.
  • No advertising or conversion pixel, and no cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • No social plug-in that loads from a third-party domain before you interact with it.
  • No fingerprinting, and no cookie syncing with a partner.

3. Accepting, refusing and withdrawing

The banner on the first visit offers accept all and reject all on the same layer and with the same visual weight, plus a per-purpose choice for anything that is not strictly necessary. Refusing costs you nothing: every page and the audit itself work identically.

To change your mind, use the cookie settings control in the footer, or clear the site data in your browser. Either route withdraws your consent as easily as it was given, as Art. 7(3) GDPR requires. The choice is stored for twelve months and then asked again.

4. Opt-out preference signals

If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal (or the Sec-GPC header), this site treats it as a refusal of every non-essential purpose and records it, without asking you to create an account. The same applies to a "Do Not Track" style signal where a browser implements it as GPC.

5. The legal basis

Storage that is strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for is permitted under § 25(2) TDDDG and needs no consent. Everything else would rest on your consent under § 25(1) TDDDG and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR — and since there is nothing else, no consent is asked for anything beyond the record of your choice. The privacy notice describes the processing in full.

6. Questions

Write to erdalbirinci@gmail.com and ask for the Data Protection Officer. A question about a cookie is answered like any other data protection request, within one month.
