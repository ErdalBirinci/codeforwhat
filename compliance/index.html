Two rules govern this table. A duty is marked applies only when the instrument's own territorial or material hook is met on the evidence in this register. And where a duty belongs to an internal record rather than a page — a risk assessment, a retention policy, a signed release — the status names the record and its owner, because a URL cannot stand in for a document that does not exist.

Evidence pack

Every artefact cited above is a static document on this site. None of them requires JavaScript, an account or a consent decision to read.

  • Privacy notice — controller, purposes, legal bases, recipients, transfers, retention, rights, US state rights, appeal, children, breach, complaint
  • Cookie policy — what is stored, what is deliberately not stored, how to withdraw
  • Accessibility statement — WCAG 2.2 AA position, known limitations, feedback and enforcement route
  • Legal notice, terms, withdrawal and complaints — imprint, pre-contract information, security contact
  • Dossier verification — recompute the hash of an issued dossier and read the rule version it was scored under

Security measures

  • TLS for every request, with HSTS and a strict transport security window; HTTP is redirected, not served.
  • Content Security Policy, frame-ancestors and a referrer policy are sent with every HTML response; no inline script is required for the legal documents to render.
  • No cookie is set for a visitor who only reads the site, so there is no session to steal and nothing to consent to before reading.
  • Payment card data is never received: checkout is a hosted page on the processor's domain.
  • Secrets live in the server environment, never in the bundle. The published bundle is checked for credentials before release.
  • A vulnerability report can be sent to the security contact in the legal notice; the handling process and the 72-hour breach decision tree are recorded internally.

Rights, complaints and accessibility feedback

  • Data subject rights — access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection — are exercised through the route in the privacy notice, answered within one month, extendable by two where the request is complex.
  • A refusal can be appealed. The appeal route is stated in the notice and is answered within 45 days.
  • A complaint can go to the supervisory authority of your habitual residence, place of work or the place of the alleged infringement; the notice names the route.
  • Accessibility feedback is answered within five working days, and the statement names the enforcement route if the answer does not resolve it.

How to check this register

Nothing on this page asks to be taken on trust. The service audits itself with the same engine it sells: the results are reproducible, the rule version is printed on every dossier, and /verify/ recomputes the hash of an issued dossier so a reader can tell whether it was altered after issue.

Where this register says out of scope, the reason is given so that an auditor can disagree with the classification rather than having to infer it. Where it says applies, the artefact is one click away. Where a duty rests on an internal record, this page says so instead of implying that a URL settles it.
