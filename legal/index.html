Terms of service

1. What is sold

One automated regulatory audit of one publicly reachable website, delivered as a PDF to the email address given at checkout. The audit issues a bounded number of GET requests, respects robots.txt beyond the landing page, submits no form and types nothing.

2. Price and payment

The price shown at checkout (currently 29,99 €) includes statutory VAT and is charged once, in advance, through the payment provider named on the checkout page. A receipt is issued for every payment; a company name and VAT identification number supplied at checkout appear on it.

3. Delivery

The dossier is delivered within 24 hours of the payment being confirmed, to the address given at checkout. If the target cannot be reached, the operator is told and the audit is re-run or the payment refunded.

4. What the audit is not

The report is evidence, not a legal opinion and not legal advice. It records what a crawler observed at a point in time, names the instrument each observation bears on, and states what it cannot see. A qualified reviewer decides what a finding means for a particular site.

5. Liability

Liability for intent and gross negligence is unlimited. For slight negligence the operator is liable only for breach of a cardinal duty, and limited to the foreseeable damage typical for this kind of contract. Liability under the Product Liability Act and for injury to life, body or health remains unaffected.

6. Applicable law

German law applies. Mandatory consumer protection of the customer's habitual residence remains unaffected.

Right of withdrawal

Consumers have a right of withdrawal of fourteen days from the conclusion of the contract. Because the audit is digital content delivered immediately, the following applies.

Expiry. The right of withdrawal expires when the operator has begun performance — the audit has been run and the dossier delivered — and the customer expressly consented to performance beginning before the withdrawal period ends, and acknowledged that doing so loses the right of withdrawal. That consent is given at checkout, before payment.

If it has not expired, withdrawal is declared by an unambiguous statement (for example an email) to erdalbirinci@gmail.com. A payment already made is returned within fourteen days of the declaration, using the same means of payment.

Privacy notice

What is processed when you buy an audit, and what is not. This notice is the Art. 13 GDPR information for the sales page and the order.

What is collected

  • The address you order an audit for and your email address. Both are required to run the audit and to deliver the dossier.
  • Optional company details (company name, VAT identification number) if you enter them, so the receipt carries them.
  • Payment data is handled by the payment provider on its own checkout page. The operator receives confirmation of payment, the amount and the email address — never card details.
  • Aggregate counts of page views and started orders, without cookies, without a client identifier and without storing an IP address, so the operator can tell whether the page works.

What is measured on the audited site

Only what any visitor's browser receives: the pages, the response headers, the cookies the server sets, the scripts loaded and the consent banner as rendered. No form is submitted, no account is created, no field is typed into. Screenshots are taken of the pages that were fetched.

Legal bases and retention

Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for the audit and its delivery; legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) for the aggregate counts; legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) for invoices, which are kept for the period tax law requires. Dossiers are stored on the operator's own engine, not in a third-party analytics service, and are deleted on request unless an invoice refers to them.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, by writing to erdalbirinci@gmail.com. You may also complain to a supervisory authority.

Cookies on this site

None. The sales page sets no cookie and loads nothing from a third party — which is also why it asks you for no consent banner.
