Terms of service

1. Scope and parties

These terms apply to every audit ordered through this site. The contract is formed with the operator named in the imprint. Terms that differ from these apply only if the operator confirms them in writing.

2. How the contract is concluded

The page describes the service; it is not an offer in the legal sense. The buyer places a binding offer by pressing the order button labelled with the payment obligation, after confirming the email address with the code sent to it and ticking the required boxes. The contract is concluded when the payment is confirmed and the order confirmation is sent. No order is accepted before that confirmation.

3. Price and payment

The price shown at checkout is the total price, including statutory VAT, and is charged once in advance. Prices for recurring plans are monthly and are charged each month until cancelled. The payment provider named at checkout processes the payment; an invoice is issued for every payment, and a company name and VAT identification number supplied at checkout are printed on it.

4. Delivery and performance

The audit begins once the payment is confirmed. The dossier is delivered to the address given at checkout within 24 hours. If the target cannot be reached, or the crawl is refused, the operator says so and either re-runs the audit or refunds the price.

5. Recurring plans and cancellation

A monitoring or agency plan runs for an indefinite period and is billed monthly in advance. It can be cancelled at any time by email to the address in the imprint, with effect from the end of the current billing month; no reason has to be given and no minimum term applies. Payments already made for a month that has begun are not refunded for a cancellation, but the service continues to the end of that month.

6. Right of withdrawal

Consumers have a right of withdrawal, described in full under Withdrawal. Cancelling a recurring plan is not a withdrawal and does not depend on it.

7. What the audit is and is not

The report is evidence, not a legal opinion and not legal advice. It records what a crawler observed at a point in time, names the instrument each observation bears on, and states what it cannot see. A qualified reviewer decides what a finding means for a particular site. The audit reads public pages only; it submits no form, creates no account and types nothing.

8. The buyer's duties

The buyer may order an audit only for a site they operate or are authorised to have examined, and confirms that at checkout. The email address given must be able to receive mail and must belong to the site's own domain, which is what the code sent to it proves. The audit must not be used to overload, probe or attack a third party's infrastructure.

9. Use of the dossier

The buyer may use the dossier within their own organisation and may pass it to their clients, auditors and advisers. The agency plan does not permit reselling the audit itself as a service under the operator's name, or presenting the engine as the buyer's own. The operator keeps the right to describe the audit method and to publish anonymised, aggregated results.

10. Defects

The audit is digital content. If it is incomplete, does not match what was ordered, or cannot be delivered, the buyer may require rectification within a reasonable period. If rectification fails, the buyer may reduce the price or terminate the contract and recover the payment. Statutory rights for digital products (§§ 327 ff. BGB) remain unaffected.

11. Liability

Liability for intent and gross negligence is unlimited. For slight negligence the operator is liable only for breach of a cardinal duty, and limited to the foreseeable damage typical for this kind of contract. Liability under the Product Liability Act and for injury to life, body or health remains unaffected.

12. Availability and changes

The operator may change the audit's scope where a legal or technical reason requires it, and will say so before the next order or the next billing month of a recurring plan. A change that materially reduces what a running plan delivers entitles the buyer to cancel it with effect from the change.

13. Applicable law

German law applies, excluding the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods. Mandatory consumer protection of the customer's habitual residence remains unaffected, including the rules on the place of jurisdiction.

14. Severability

Right of withdrawal

Your right

If you are a consumer, you have the right to withdraw from this contract within fourteen days without giving any reason. The period begins on the day the contract is concluded. To exercise it, tell the operator by an unambiguous statement — for example an email to erdalbirinci@gmail.com — before the period ends. The model form below may be used but is not required.

When the right ends for this service

The audit is digital content delivered immediately, so the right of withdrawal ends when the operator has fully performed — the audit has run and the dossier has been delivered — and you expressly agreed before performance began that it should begin, and acknowledged that full performance ends your right of withdrawal. § 356(5) BGB. The checkout asks for exactly that agreement, in a box of its own, before payment; the time it was given is recorded with the order.

If you withdraw

A payment already made is returned within fourteen days of the declaration, using the same means of payment and at no cost to you. If you asked for performance to begin during the withdrawal period and then withdraw, you pay a proportionate amount for the part of the service already performed — for this audit, the share of the work done before the declaration, which the operator will state.

Recurring plans

Withdrawal ends the plan; it is not the same as cancelling it. A monthly plan can also be cancelled at any time under clause 5 of the terms, and cancelling is the simpler route once the first audit has been delivered.

Accessibility statement

This statement is the information required by § 14 of the German Accessibility Strengthening Act (BFSG) and by the European Accessibility Act.

How accessible this site is

This site aims to conform to WCAG 2.2 level AA. It is built to be operable by keyboard alone, with visible focus, a skip link, text alternatives for every image that carries meaning, a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for body text, no content that flashes, and no information conveyed by colour alone. Motion is optional: every animation stops for a reader who asks their system for reduced motion, and every film has a readable equivalent.

Known limitations

Every page that ships is loaded in Chrome by the build's own smoke test, which fails on a console error, on text below a 4.5:1 contrast ratio, and on anything cut off at 200% zoom — the checks a browser can make on its own. What that pass cannot see is a screen reader walking the page, so a manual audit with NVDA and VoiceOver is the next step, and any finding from it is recorded here with the page it affects and the date it will be fixed.

Feedback and enforcement

If something on this site is not accessible to you, write to erdalbirinci@gmail.com with the page and what happened. The answer names what will change and when. If the response does not resolve it, you may turn to the market surveillance authority of the Land Berlin responsible for the BFSG under § 28 BFSG.
